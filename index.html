How to make Mia's Favorite Slime!!

Ingredients needed:

  • 1 Medium sized bowl to put the ingredients in
  • 1 Spatula or spoon to mix ingredients with
  • 1 Tsp Borax
  • 1/2 Cup Warm Water
  • 1 Cup Clear liquid glue

Step 1:

Create your "activator solution" by thoroughly mixing together 1 tsp borax into 1/2 cup of warm water.

Step 2:

Pour your glue and activator solution into your bowl.

Step 3:

Mix all of your ingredients together. Add in your activator solution little by little as you go.

Step 4:

Once the slime is no longer too sticky, you'll know that it is ready and you can then play with your slime!
